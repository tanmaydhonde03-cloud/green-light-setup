Portfolio money management rules
  • Every attempt has a maximum planned loss equal to your saved risk-per-trade setting, before transaction costs and slippage.
  • Each asset has its own four-attempt limit. Four attempts on Gold do not use GBPUSD's four-attempt allowance.
  • Maximum planned risk per asset/day is your risk setting × 4. Across N assets actually traded, tracked capacity is N × your risk setting × 4.
  • Position size is calculated from: Lot size = your risk per trade ÷ (SL distance in pips × pip value per lot in USD).
  • Use the broker's permitted lot step and round position size down, so planned risk does not exceed your selected risk amount.
  • Correlated positions can still create concentrated exposure. The dashboard reports total exposure but does not automatically calculate correlation.

Data integrity
This application calculates statistics from manually entered trades. It does not automatically download historical FX candles or simulate missing trades.
